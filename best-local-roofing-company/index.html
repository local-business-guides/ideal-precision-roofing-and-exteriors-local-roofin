What the best local roofing company should explain

A useful roofing conversation starts with the condition of your roof, not a one-size-fits-all recommendation. Ask what the inspection found, which areas need attention and whether repair or replacement is being proposed. A clear explanation helps you understand the work before you make a decision.

A best local roofing company should also be ready to discuss materials and the scope of the job in everyday language. For a home, that could mean talking through asphalt shingles or metal roofing. Commercial properties may need a different system, such as TPO or EPDM.

What roofing work covers and how the process works

Roofing services include inspections, repairs and replacements. Available systems include asphalt shingles and metal roofing for residential work, along with commercial TPO and EPDM. Related exterior services include gutters, siding, exterior painting and storm damage repair.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- What it covers: Roof condition checks, repairs, replacements and several exterior services.
- Typical timing: The inspection is the first step. How long the full project takes depends on the roof, the work involved and other project details, so ask for a schedule specific to your property.
- What affects cost: Roof size and condition, chosen material, repair needs and the scope of installation can all affect the estimate. The estimate is the place to review the proposed work and materials, not just a total.

Compare providers by checking that each has inspected the roof, described the same work and identified the materials included. Ask how storm damage will be recorded and what cleanup and final review involve.

A few terms you may hear: drone inspection means roof viewing with a drone; TPO and EPDM are types of commercial roofing systems; scope means the work included in a proposal. Ask the contractor to explain any other terms that aren't familiar.
